# Hero kit references — the blade, the look, the tools, the pact

What the designer named, checked against sources on 2026-10-17. Summaries only.

## The cursed blade: Elden Ring Nightreign's Executor
- The Executor's skill (Cursed Sword; one guide calls it Suncatcher) draws a large cursed blade and puts you in a defensive
  stance with its own combo and extra poise damage.
- **Each well-timed deflect charges the blade; after five, it is empowered and the skill becomes a quick slash for massive
  damage and stagger.** The passive (name varies by guide) rewards perfect parries with a stronger blade.
- **Correction (the designer's screenshots, 2026-10-17):** the blade *is* wrapped: twisted, knotted bark-like strips bind its
  length. My search found no source for this and I wrongly called it the designer's own vision. In the second screenshot the
  blade is lit gold with a trailing streak and drifting embers: the charge shown on the weapon. The same frames show a mood worth
  noting: a deep indigo night, a vivid purple-and-cyan moon, warm copper armour, gold light, sparks.
- Design read: a charge you earn by fighting well, shown on the weapon itself rather than on a meter. A cursed object whose
  power is *visible* is the right kind of story-and-system object.

## The look: Ryu Hayabusa's older blue outfit
- In the early *Ninja Gaiden* games Ryu wears a **blue ninja outfit** (sources differ between "blue" and "purple/blue"; the
  NES one is often drawn with red accents), with the face fully masked in his classic figure form. The 2004 reboot replaced it
  with an all-black look. He carries the Dragon Sword.
- Design read: a classic silhouette (mask, long wrapped limbs, a scarf or tail) in cool blue gives us a hero who reads at a
  glance and is not black-on-black. It also gives the **blade's aura a clear contrast colour**, and it must not be red or
  yellow, which belong to enemy attacks (D010).

## The pact: Wyll in Baldur's Gate 3
- Wyll's pact is with a devil (Mizora), who tricked him into signing; it was **not presented as a free choice**. When his
  father came home and found him with a devil, he demanded an explanation, and Wyll could not give one: his father cast him
  out of the city.
- Design read: the exile beat in the designer's pitch has a direct model, and the idea that the pact was *not entirely free*
  is already in the source. We lean on it (D031).

## The essence: Rose Belmont's Arcana
- Defeated bosses are sealed into tarot cards that grant spells. They also *talk* to Rose afterwards, some kind, some mocking.
  See `belmonts-curse.md`.

## The tools and the traversal tool
- **Silksong's tools** (the model the designer named): thrown and placed items, used alongside the needle, for extra damage
  or utility; Silksong's crests change the move set. Details: `../hollow-shinobi/design-research/01` §5.
- **Ghost of Tsushima's "ghost weapons"**: kunai (stagger, interrupts), smoke bombs, sticky and black-powder bombs,
  firecrackers, blowgun darts. A ready list of ninja-appropriate tools.
- **Sekiro's prosthetic**: a grapple for traversal and a set of shinobi tools on one arm. Details: `05` §1.5, §1.8.
- **Belmont's Curse's whip** (swing, zip to rings, latch onto enemies). The latch can feel unreliable: targeting must be solid.
- Ninja-native candidates for our traversal tool (to choose in 0.4): a kunai on a chain (kusari), a chain-and-sickle
  (kusarigama), a grappling hook (kaginawa).
