# 0.1 — Feel and references

The designer's answers, 2026-10-17, organised. Draft pillars at the end are proposals, not yet confirmed.

## The three feelings (every decision is tested against these)
1. **Genuine curiosity.** While playing, you want to explore and to learn more about the story.
2. **Excitement at the new.** Each new area and boss is thrilling because the challenge is great.
3. **Euphoria at the end of a boss.** When the controller goes down: "Wow, that was a really fun time."

## Touchstones and what we take from each
| Touchstone | What we take |
|---|---|
| **Hollow Knight** | Iconic areas, each with its own music; bosses with their own music, moves and phases; the combat feel (dash, spacing, pogo, mostly hitting); a world you re-read once you have new tools |
| **Dark Souls / Elden Ring** | The same: iconic places and bosses; moves that change and add in later phases; discovery as the engine of curiosity; challenge that ends in euphoria |
| **God of War (recent) / Ghost of Tsushima** | The parry as a reward for reading hard-to-time attacks (delayed, fast, odd-looking): a successful parry opens the enemy for a lot of damage |
| **Castlevania: Belmont's Curse** | The art direction and how characters move and function; a whip that is also the traversal tool (our ninja finds an equivalent). Notes: `references/belmonts-curse.md` |
| **Metroidvania in general** | Abilities as keys: double jump, wall run, wall cling, a grapple-like tool. Each reopens old areas and opens new ones |
| **Story: "blend from many iconic games"** | Named so far: Wyll's pact and exile (Baldur's Gate 3), the Executor's blade (Nightreign), Rose's Arcana (Belmont's Curse), a hero not fully human whose life was an experiment (Ichigo), humans who pact with demons for power (Doom 2016), a revelation, a death and a climb out of hell (Kratos and Zeus), a beaten people who bow (the Mantis Lords), foreshadowing (Attack on Titan, the Red Wedding). Details: `references/hero-kit-references.md` |

## Combat in one paragraph (as stated; details in 0.4)
Hollow Knight-like: dash, spacing, pogo, and mostly hitting. A **hold-and-release attack** (hold Square; standing or in the
air; release for a charged attack). Abilities specific to this hero on top. Enemy attacks come in two colours: **red** must be
dodged or jumped; **yellow** can be parried. Parry works the God of War / Ghost of Tsushima way: the attacks are hard to time,
and a clean parry leaves a big opening.

## Scope
A very good **playable demo**: a good number of the features, variety in the world, and one or two bosses (a mini-boss and a
main boss). Belmont's Curse itself shipped a one-chapter demo.

## Draft pillars (for confirmation)
1. **Curiosity first.** Every place and every character raises a question the player wants answered; the story is found, in fragments.
2. **Iconic places, iconic fights.** Each area and each boss has its own face and its own music.
3. **Hard, readable, euphoric.** A challenge that rewards learning: red and yellow tell you what to do, each boss figure has one
   rhythm you can learn, and the win feels earned.
4. **A body that is a pleasure to move.** Melee, dash, pogo, a charged strike and a traversal tool are one language, used in
   fights and in exploration alike.
5. **Show, don't tell, and let people talk.** No tutorials or event pop-ups; the world says what it can. NPCs and bosses speak
   (D017), and story is found in fragments the player chooses to read.
