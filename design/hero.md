# 0.2 — The hero: Kaname

His name is **Kaname (要)** (D038): the pin that holds a folding fan together, the point everything hinges on. It reads as an
ordinary name, and it is a clue hidden in plain sight. Three parts here: locked, leaning, and Claude's proposals.
References: `references/hero-kit-references.md`.

## Locked
- **Look (D020):** a classical ninja in the vein of Ryu Hayabusa's older blue outfit; super cool.
- **Face and arms (D084, amended by D103):** his face is covered by a mask, with a narrow eye slit so the eyes can carry the expression and the dialogue
  portrait. **He has no visible arms, as in Little Shinobi (D103):** the sword simply appears in his hand. In the story the curse is still **only his right
  forearm**; on screen it shows as the violet glow at the hilt and on the blade.
  My palette note: nothing on him is red or yellow, since those belong to the combat language (D010).
- **Headband and scarf (D090, D091):** a cloth band wrapped round his head just above the eyes (Little Shinobi's look), now **in the blade's dark purple**; the scarf is its
  tail, always tied to the back of the head. No tail on the hood, no smoke. **The designer chose look A (D095):** the band and a short tail in purple (the band-only and knight-silhouette looks were set aside; `concepts/kaname-looks-A-B-C.png`).
- **Training (D020):** trained in the way of the shinobi, but fairly new; he does not yet have all the tools.
- **Personality (D024):** restrained and dutiful on the surface. **Sarcasm and humour are how he copes** with what has happened
  to him. More of him opens up as the story goes on.
- **He hates the curse (D039):** he hates that part of himself, and the choice he made, though he felt it was the only thing he
  could do. Yet **consuming bosses' essences for new abilities and attacks is a core gameplay mechanic** (D022): the thing he
  hates is what the player loves to use.
- **His arc (D040):** beyond the betrayal and the revenge, the story is about **acceptance**: his growth in coming to terms with
  what he is and seeing the power as a gift.
- **What drives him (D028):** he cares about the people close to him and will do anything to keep his family safe: that is why he
  takes the pact.
- **The two arms (D025):** the **right arm** is scarred and is the only arm that can hold the cursed blade. The **left arm** throws
  shuriken and holds the rope or whip for traversal. Taught on one side, made on the other.
- **Movement (D096, D100):** run, jump, **dash (tap)** and **sprint (hold dash)**, one dash per jump in the air; and a quick **sword strike** (forward, up, or down in the air), Hollow Knight-style, no combo chain; a down strike that lands is a **pogo** (D105), and any blow landed in the air gives back the air dash; **hold the strike button to charge and let go to draw** (D107: a flat lunge cut, the iai draw of P13); a **parry** with a short window, a **health bar** of 5, and a dash that is untouchable (D109, proposals). Numbers are in `CLAUDE.md`; the designer tunes them by hand.
- **Tools (D027):** limited use, refilled when he rests.
- **Family (D026, D042, D048, D057):** a father on the clan's council (dead before the midpoint), a mother, and a **younger sibling
  who always supports him**. The Elder, his mentor and a father figure, is at home too. **The mother gives him the key** to the
  sanctum when the clan falls. The Elder, his old mentor, is at home too.
- **The world's view of him changes (D041):** early on, the rival clans and ordinary people see a threat, an evil thing. When he
  returns to save people and to fight the clan that betrayed him, the world begins to see a hopeful spirit instead of a scary
  demon.

## The blade (locked)
- **The cursed blade (D021):** a demonic artifact taken on through a pact; its aura builds a charge as he deals damage and parries;
  he tries to throw it away and it returns to its sheath; only the scarred arm can hold it.
- **The seals (D036):** its wrappings are sealing tags that burn away as the charge builds. *(The drawings now show the katana plain, because
  white tags looked bad in that art style (D085). The seals stand as an idea; how they look is open. One option: dark bands carrying glyphs
  that light up violet as the charge builds.)*
- **The glow (D037): deep violet-black, with black smoke, and it pulses.** The red is gone: red belongs to danger alone (D010), so
  a player never has to ask what a red thing means.
- **Compared with the Executor** in your screenshots (a blade bound in twisted strips, lit gold with a trailing streak and
  embers): ours has paper seals instead of strips, and a violet-black pulse and smoke in place of gold.

## How the curse shows (D062)
**The price is emotional, not mechanical (D062).** You said the blade's power should "cost something" and that the
end is his to decide. So the blade stays a pure reward in play; the cost is *what it does to him*, and it is shown, not told:
- **Consuming an essence is never triumphant (D062).** A short wordless beat each time: his shoulders drop, he flexes or covers the
  scarred forearm, his eyes narrow above the mask, or he makes a joke that does not land. Over the game the beat changes: revulsion,
  then resignation, then ease. The player gets a new power while his eyes and body say what it costs, and his acceptance is visible
  in a moment the player sees dozens of times.
- **The curse stays on the right forearm (D084).** It never spreads past it. With each essence its markings deepen and glow: visible
  on him and in his portrait. *How much the drawing shows is secondary (D092): dialogue and story carry the fact that he is cursed. With no visible arms (D103) it shows as the glow on the hilt and the blade.*
- **People's reactions follow his deeds, not his scar.** Early: they flinch. Later, after he has saved people: they look at him
  with hope. (This replaces my earlier idea that NPCs flinch more as the scar spreads.)
- **The glow can change with him.** Same violet-black, but the smoke gains pale lights as he comes to accept it: the *hopeful
  spirit* of D041 as something you can see on his blade.
- **The made speak (D062, see `story.md`).** Each essence is a voice in his head; over time they become a chorus.

**P13 — The draw.** The blade lives in its sheath and always returns, so the hold-and-release attack (D009) can be an **iai draw**:
a cut from the sheath. The sheath becomes a mechanic.

**P08b — Two reward tracks.** Demon essence (right hand) and shinobi arts or tools (left hand); a rival clan can be the source of
the second.

**Possible later ability:** *throw and recall*. The blade he cannot throw away becomes a returning weapon.

## Personality, as lines (calibration only, not a script)
- *To his mother, dutiful:* "I'll hold the gate."
- *To a demon, dry:* "Another one. They did say they'd come in waves. Nobody said they'd be rude about it."
- *After the exile:* "Banished by my own family. They could at least have packed me a lunch."
- *The crack in it:* a joke that does not land, and the silence after.
